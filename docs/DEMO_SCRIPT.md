# Demo click path (10–12 min)

A tap-by-tap path through all 8 user stories. Each step says **what to tap** and **what it proves**.
The words you say are yours to write; the right column is only a cue.

**Before you present**
1. Open **hmm-lilac-xi.vercel.app** → login screen → **↺ Start the demo over**. This gives a fresh Mouna, Ines with
   her history, the default rules, and fresh sample data.
2. Optional: install it on your phone (Add to Home Screen) and mirror the phone.
3. Put the join codes on a slide: `CALC-7Q2K` · `MICRO-4M8T` · `SAD-9H3P`.

**Edge states you can show if asked**
- **Insights hidden:** the professor opens a course with fewer than 5 students.
- **Wrong password:** sign in with a wrong password.
- **Forgot password:** use the link on the login screen.
- **Empty home:** a new classmate account before joining a course.
- **AI unavailable:** "hmm. can't think right now, your session is saved" appears if the AI is down. It retries on its own first.
- **Keeps asking for the answer:** step 3.

**Classmates live:** they tap *Create your account* with their @aui.ma email and join with a code from the slide.
After 5+ of them finish a session, the professor view includes them.

**If something goes wrong**
- **AI is slow or busy:** wait about 5 seconds; it retries on its own. Have screenshots in `docs/screenshots/live/` as a backup.
- **The app looks old:** reload once (Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+R on a computer; close and reopen on a phone).
